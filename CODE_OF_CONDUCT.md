# QuiverDL Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in QuiverDL a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Give actionable feedback and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect different experience levels and help newcomers learn.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its community.
- Credit other people's work and ideas appropriately.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith argument
- Publishing private information without permission
- Any conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation,
or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates these standards. Enforcement decisions
will consider context, impact, and whether behavior is repeated.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner using the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Sheekovic). Do not open a public issue about a
private conduct report. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential to the extent
possible.

## Scope

This code applies in QuiverDL repositories, Discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces
where someone is representing the project.
